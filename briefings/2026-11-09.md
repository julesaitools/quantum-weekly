**The essence:** Q3 earnings split the field — IonQ, Quantinuum and IQM raised guidance while Pasqal's revenue fell 42%. The UK put £138M of procurement money behind quantum sensing. IBM anchored Nighthawk into a regional ecosystem in Chicago. The EU Quantum Act is tentatively on the Commission's agenda for 18 November. Two near-term actions: EuroHPC quantum calls close 17 November, and a new NL–UK TechBridge quantum call opened 2 November.

## 1. Top signals

### 1.1 Q3 results split the sector

*5–7 Nov — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)*

- **What:** IonQ: $173.8M Q3 revenue (+336%), FY guidance raised to $505–515M, backlog $1.2B. Quantinuum: $9.1M (+233%), FY outlook raised to $31–34M. IQM: €8.86M (+51%), €42.2M order intake, €102.5M backlog, €33M LUMI contract. Pasqal: €3.3M, down from €5.7M, because it sold no QPUs; services up 53% to €3.1M; €312.8M cash, €69.8M booked-and-awarded.
- **Why it matters:** much of IonQ's figure comes from acquisitions, not quantum computing. Pasqal's number exposes the European model's dependence on lumpy one-off system sales. For hardware builders, backlog and order intake are the real metric, not quarterly revenue.

### 1.2 UK commits £138M to quantum sensing procurement

*6 Nov — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/06/uk-announces-new-funding-to-support-quantum-technology-commercialisation/)*

- **What:** £138M for cross-government quantum sensing procurement (incl. £30M MoD); £12M EPSRC for a Quantum Commercialisation Skills Centre run by the UK Quantum Super Connector (Imperial, Bristol, Cambridge, Glasgow, Oxford); £35M Innovate UK for two quantum networking testbed calls launching February 2027; a Defence Quantum Action Plan with details due early 2027. UKRI reported £300M public investment 2014–2024 produced 74 spinouts and ~£482M private capital.
- **Why it matters:** this is procurement, not research subsidy — the state as first customer. That is the mechanism quantum sensing has been missing and the biggest structural difference between the UK and continental approaches.
- **Relevance:** the Netherlands has strong sensing research but no comparable demand instrument. A concrete argument for a Dutch/regional launching-customer approach.

### 1.3 IBM anchors Nighthawk into a regional ecosystem in Chicago

*5–6 Nov — [IBM newsroom (Chicago)](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-11-06-ibm-unveils-its-most-advanced-quantum-computer-in-chicago,-marking-a-milestone-for-illinoiss-quantum-future) · [IBM newsroom (ORNL/AMD)](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-11-05-oak-ridge-national-lab,-ibm,-and-amd-launch-new-co-design-platform-for-quantum-error-correction)*

- **What:** on-premises Quantum System Two with the 120-qubit Nighthawk (218 couplers) in Chicago with the University of Chicago and the State of Illinois, tied to a 750-jobs goal. A day earlier, IBM, Oak Ridge National Lab and AMD launched a quantum error correction co-design platform targeting the DOE goal of 100 logical qubits and 100,000 operations by late 2028. Yonsei University took the first international on-premises Nighthawk r2.
- **Why it matters:** IBM is converting hardware placements into regional industrial policy. The ORNL/AMD platform is the more significant item — error correction is becoming a joint quantum/classical-accelerator engineering problem, which puts control electronics, interconnect and HPC integration on the critical path.
- **Relevance:** the hard part is the integration layer, not the qubits — which is where Brainport's strength and realistic value capture sit.

### 1.4 QuiX Quantum positions inside the €50M P4Q photonics pilot line

*4 Nov — [QuiX Quantum](https://www.quixquantum.com/news/quix-quantum-advances-europes-industrial-quantum-computing-ambitions)*

- **What:** QuiX Quantum (Enschede/Amsterdam/Stuttgart/Ulm) set out its role as industrial user in Photonics for Quantum (P4Q): €50M, co-funded by the EU Chips JU and national authorities, 29 partners across twelve countries, coordinated by the University of Twente, April 2026 to March 2029. QuiX feeds in manufacturing requirements — optical loss reduction, phase control, reproducibility — and plans loss-tolerant logical-qubit demos for its Dedalo architecture in 2028–2030.
- **Caveat:** P4Q itself launched in January 2026; this is company positioning, not a new programme. Its value is the specific bottleneck list, which is effectively a supplier requirements document.
- **Relevance:** the most directly Brainport-relevant item of the week — these are PhotonDelta-ecosystem problems, with a named industrial pull customer and a 2029 horizon.

### 1.5 EU Quantum Act tentatively scheduled for 18 November

*[MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2531216/digital-fairness-quantum-acts-appear-on-eu-commission-s-november-agenda) · [European Parliament Legislative Train](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-new-plan-for-europe-s-sustainable-prosperity-and-competitiveness/file-quantum-act)*

- **What:** the Commission's tentative agenda places a Quantum Act proposal on 18 November 2026. Originally slated for Q2 2026; the Legislative Train still shows it as a planned initiative, not a proposal. I could not verify a confirmation from this week, and the reporting notes the schedule can change — treat the date as indicative.
- **Why it matters:** expected to cover coordination of national programmes, pilot lines and infrastructure access, possibly procurement and sovereignty requirements. If it lands, it frames European quantum industrial policy for the rest of the decade.
- **Relevance:** worth having a short regional position ready on pilot-line access and supply-chain scope.

### 1.6 UK workplace pension capital enters quantum for the first time

*2 Nov — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/scottish-widows-and-aberdeen-allocate-uk-workplace-pension-capital-to-oxford-quantum-circuits-and-quantum-motion-via-ltaf/)*

- **What:** Scottish Widows, with Aberdeen, allocated UK workplace pension capital to Oxford Quantum Circuits and Quantum Motion through a Long-Term Asset Fund. Amounts undisclosed.
- **Why it matters:** a genuinely new capital class — patient, large, and entering via a vehicle built to let DC schemes hold illiquid assets. The signal is the precedent, not the volume.
- **Relevance:** late-stage capital is the binding constraint on Dutch quantum scale-ups. Whether Dutch pension funds could do something equivalent is a question for Invest-NL and InnovationQuarter.

### 1.7 Error correction keeps compounding

*5–7 Nov — [Quantum Computing Report (Quantinuum)](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-demonstrates-99-9995-logical-memory-fidelity-on-helios-trapped-ion-processor/) · [Quantum Computing Report (Riverlane)](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/riverlane-demonstrates-data-driven-dem-augmentation-to-reduce-surface-code-logical-errors/)*

- **What:** Quantinuum reported 99.9995% logical memory fidelity on Helios. Riverlane showed data-driven surface-code error-detection model augmentation, cutting logical error rates up to 75% in simulation and 14% on hardware. Alice & Bob revised their RSA-2048 estimate to ~23,000 cat qubits at 12 MW, down from roughly a million qubits and 500 MW. IBM Quantum and QuICS used AI agents to find 500+ lower-resource magic-state factories.
- **Why it matters:** resource estimates for cryptographically relevant quantum computing keep falling, and much of the gain now comes from software, decoding and compilation rather than better qubits. Hardware-only timelines are becoming unreliable.
- **Relevance:** strengthens the case for treating post-quantum migration as near-term operational work for regional industry and public bodies. Europol issued the same warning this week.

### 1.8 NL–Taiwan photonics projects selected; QuTech runs CFD on real hardware

*4–5 Nov — [Bits&Chips](https://bits-chips.com/article/four-dutch-taiwanese-projects-target-scalable-photonics/) · [QuTech](https://qutech.nl/newsroom/news/)*

- **What:** four projects were selected under the Netherlands–Taiwan TechBridge call on photonic integrated circuits. Separately, TU Delft's Quantum-CFD Lab ran fluid-dynamics quantum algorithms on QuTech hardware via Quantum Inspire — on actual Tuna chips, not simulators.
- **Relevance:** both sit in the Brainport/PhotonDelta orbit. The CFD work has an obvious regional industrial audience in aerospace, automotive and high-tech systems.

## 2. Opportunities and calls

### EuroHPC JU — six quantum calls

*Deadline: 17 November 2026, 17:00 CET — [EuroHPC JU](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/six-new-quantum-calls-launched-eurohpc-joint-undertaking-2026-08-14_en) · [NCP Flanders](https://ncpflanders.be/news/eurohpc-launches-new-quantum-calls-update)*

- ~€119M total: Neutral-Atom Platform Technologies (cloud-accessible processors up to 10,000 physical qubits), Superconducting Platform Technologies, Trapped-Ion Platform Technologies, Next-Generation QKD Systems, Quantum Experimental Pilot Lines, Quantum-Testing Infrastructure. 3.5-year projects.
- The last two are most relevant for a manufacturing-and-test region. One week out — too late to form a consortium, not too late to find out whether Brainport parties are in one.

### RVO TechBridge: United Kingdom Call 2026 — Quantum Technologies

*Open 2 November 2026 to 9 February 2027, 17:00 CET — [RVO](https://english.rvo.nl/subsidies-financing/techbridge/uk-call-2026-quantum-technologies)*

- Newly opened this week. €1.2M total; max €300,000 per project per Dutch consortium; projects up to 24 months; for developing, prototyping and testing second-generation quantum technology.
- The Dutch lead applicant must be a company, at least one Dutch company required, at least one UK company required (UK partners apply to UKRI). Funding rates: research 45% SME / 35% industry / 50% universities; development 35% SME / 25% industry / 25% universities.
- Small amounts, but a three-month window and — given the UK's £138M sensing procurement and February 2027 networking testbeds — a sensible route for Brainport companies into UK demand-side programmes.

## 3. Capital, companies, and talent

### M&A

- **IonQ closes $1.15B acquisition of Elisity** *(4 Nov — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/04/ionq-completes-acquisition-zero-trust-security-company-elisity/))*: a Zero Trust network security company — continuing its strategy of buying into cybersecurity revenue rather than growing it from quantum hardware. A large part of why IonQ's revenue line looks the way it does.
- **Horizon Quantum signs LOI to acquire Entropica Labs for ~$27M** *(5 Nov — [The Qubit Report](https://thequbitreport.com/industry-business/2026/11/05/horizon-quantums-proposed-acquisition-of-entropica-labs-benefits-and-risks-analysis/))*: all-equity. All-equity signals consolidation driven by capability gaps rather than cash; expect more among quantum software firms.

### Funding

- **Quint Computing raises €9.1M** *(3 Nov — [tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/11/03/quint-computing-raises-eur91m-to-scale-quantum-computing-technology/))*: FAU Erlangen spinout, Munich Quantum Valley; reported as the largest first round for a German quantum computing company, for error-corrected processor architectures; backed by Quantonation.
- **Scottish Widows / Aberdeen pension allocation via LTAF** *(2 Nov)*: see 1.6.
- **IQM order book** *(5 Nov — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/05/iqm-books-e42-2-million-in-orders-as-quarterly-revenue-rises-51-in-q3-2026/))*: €42.2M order intake, €102.5M backlog, €33M LUMI AI Factory contract — the strongest European order book in the sector.

### Products and partnerships

- **Universal Quantum launches the iQPU** *(3 Nov — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/03/universal-quantum-combines-ion-trap-controls-and-modular-links-in-new-chip/))*: integrating ion trap, control electronics and modular links on one chip, with DLR contracts behind it. A packaging/integration play rather than a qubit-count claim.
- **Quantinuum Nexus roadmap** *(5 Nov)*: automating fault-tolerant application deployment through 2029, targeting the Apollo machine.
- **IQM and Volkswagen** *(5 Nov)*: up to a 124× reduction in classical execution time on a 50-orbital battery-chemistry benchmark using quantum-classical AFQMC. Industrial-partner validation of this kind is still rare.

### Talent

- **Leadership moves** *(2–7 Nov — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/whos-news-strategic-appointments-at-quantum-emotion-qtonic-quantum-chicago-quantum-exchange-and-equal1/))*: Chicago Quantum Exchange appointed Aaron Fluitt as CEO; Equal1 appointed Andrew Flynn as COO; Qtonic Quantum appointed a former NATO Allied Land Command chief as head of operations; Quantum eMotion also named new leadership.
- The Qtonic hire is the one to read into: senior military leadership moving into quantum commercial roles tracks the defence procurement money now visible in the UK and in EuroHPC's inertial-navigation challenge. Defence is becoming a demand channel, not just a research funder.

## 4. Possible follow-up actions

1. **EuroHPC, this week.** The six quantum calls close 17 November. Too late to build a consortium, but worth a quick check with QDNL, PhotonDelta and the regional knowledge institutes on whether Brainport parties are in any of the pilot-line or testing-infrastructure proposals — and if not, treating that as a finding for the next round.
2. **TechBridge UK quantum call — matchmaking, next 4–6 weeks.** €300k per consortium is small, but the call needs a Dutch company lead and a UK partner, and runs to 9 February. Given the UK's new £138M sensing procurement and February 2027 networking testbeds, this is the cheapest route into UK demand-side programmes. Candidate approach: identify 3–5 regional sensing/photonics companies and pair them with UK partners before the Christmas break.
3. **EU Quantum Act — prepare a short position before 18 November.** The date is indicative and may slip, but if a proposal appears, the useful moment is the first two weeks. One or two pages on pilot-line access and supply-chain scope from a manufacturing-region perspective beats a reactive response in January.
4. **QuiX Quantum / P4Q — concrete supplier conversation.** QuiX has publicly named its manufacturing bottlenecks (optical loss, phase control, reproducibility at scale) against a 2029 pilot-line horizon. That is an unusually specific requirements list. Worth a direct conversation with QuiX and the University of Twente about which of those problems Brainport suppliers could take on.
5. **Late-stage capital — worth a real question, not an assumption.** The Scottish Widows LTAF allocation is the first time workplace pension capital has entered quantum. Whether a Dutch equivalent is possible, and through what vehicle, is a question for Invest-NL and InnovationQuarter. This is the binding constraint on Dutch quantum scale-ups.
