*Strategic signals for Brainport Development*

**The essence of the week.** Intel is putting about $2.3 billion on the table for Israel's Classiq — the largest quantum acquisition to date, and the clearest signal that semiconductor incumbents want to own the full quantum stack rather than buy in later. In addition, NIST has finalised the binding migration schedule for post-quantum cryptography, moving quantum security from roadmap to procurement requirement. For Brainport the concrete relevance lies mainly in the underlying layers: foundry access, photonic switching, test infrastructure — and a €119 million EuroHPC deadline that is now fifteen days away.

## 1. Top signals

### 1. Intel acquires Classiq for ~$2.3 billion

*26 October — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/intel-to-acquire-classiq/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/intel-to-buy-israeli-startup-classiq-in-quantum-computing-push) · [Intel newsroom](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/corporate/intel-classiq-accelerate-scalable-quantum-computing.html)*

- **What:** definitive agreement, cash and shares, completion expected in the first half of 2027 subject to regulators. Classiq automates quantum algorithm synthesis; Intel combines this with its own silicon spin qubits and cryogenic control electronics. Intel says it will keep Classiq hardware-agnostic.
- **Why it matters:** the largest deal in the sector ever, and notably Intel is buying the *software* layer, not hardware. That suggests Intel considers its own spin qubit path strong enough and sees the toolchain as the scarce asset. It also puts a price tag on the software layer that many European players occupy.
- **Relevance:** silicon spin qubits are CMOS-compatible — that affects the Dutch and Brabant semiconductor chain more directly than superconducting or neutral-atom routes. At the same time a consolidation signal: European quantum software companies are becoming acquisition candidates. The question for ecosystem strategy is whether we retain value or export it.

### 2. NIST finalises IR 8547 — hard PQC deadlines 2030/2035

*27 October — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/nist-releases-final-ir-8547-establishing-definitive-post-quantum-cryptography-transition-timeline-and-deprecation-schedules/) · [Cloud Security Alliance](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-nist-ir8547-pqc-transition-20261030-csa-st/)*

- **What:** quantum-vulnerable algorithms will be deprecated after 2030 and disallowed after 2035. Federal high-value systems face tighter dates in 2030 and 2031.
- **Why it matters:** this is de facto the global clock. Suppliers to US governments or their supply chains will get contractual requirements out of this, not just advice.
- **Relevance:** combine this with the Dutch government-wide Quantum Strategy of 5 October, which announces quantum-safe requirements in central government procurement by the end of 2028 at the latest ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/05/kabinet-nederland-in-2035-leidend-met-quantum-en-weerbaar-tegen-risicos)), and concrete, near-term market demand emerges. For Brainport the sharpest angle is not quantum computing but *crypto-agility* at machine builders, OT and embedded suppliers with product lifetimes of 10–20 years. That is a regional programme topic, now.

### 3. Alice & Bob halves the RSA-2048 estimate

*reported 30 October — [Alice & Bob](https://alice-bob.com/blog/our-estimate-for-factoring-rsa-2048-with-cat-qubits-pushing-the-frontier-between-size-and-speed)*

- **What:** about 23,000 cat qubits could factor RSA-2048 in roughly three days — roughly half of earlier resource estimates.
- **Why it matters:** resource estimates are not demonstrations, but the trend is unambiguous: the threshold moves down every year. That is the analytical foundation under signal 2, and it shortens the safe planning horizon.

### 4. EuroHPC: €119 million in six quantum calls closes 17 November

*deadline 17 November 2026 — [EuroHPC JU](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/six-new-quantum-calls-launched-eurohpc-joint-undertaking-2026-08-14_en) · [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/24/eurohpc-119m-funding-calls-quantum-technologies/)*

- **What:** platform technology for trapped ions / superconducting / neutral atoms (€20M each), next-generation QKD (€24M), pan-European test and certification infrastructure (€20M), experimental pilot lines TRL 4–6 (€15M). Project duration about 3.5 years.
- **Why it matters:** this is probably the last big platform money before the Quantum Act redraws the toolkit.
- **Relevance:** QTI-13 (test infrastructure) and QEXP-14 (pilot lines) are closest to Dutch and Brabant strengths — test and measurement, cryogenic components, packaging, photonics. Fifteen days is too short to lead, but not too short to join as a partner.

### 5. SkyWater opens foundry access for quantum startups (OnRamp)

*reported 30 October — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/skywater-technology-introduces-onramp-foundry-program-and-details-sp90-200-mm-silicon-nitride-quantum-photonics-platform/) · [SkyWater](https://www.skywatertechnology.com/skywater-technology-launches-onramp-to-support-the-next-generation-of-quantum-innovators/)*

- **What:** a programme giving quantum teams structured access to chip fabrication, including a 200 mm silicon nitride quantum photonics platform (SP90).
- **Why it matters:** fab access is the real bottleneck for quantum hardware startups, not capital. The US is now turning it into a commercial service.
- **Relevance:** this is exactly the proposition Europe is trying to build via CHIPS JU pilot lines, but in a more fragmented way. For Brainport the question is whether regional pilot line, test and packaging capacity can be explicitly positioned as a "European on-ramp" — with PhotonDelta and the TU/e photonics base as the starting point.

### 6. European hardware delivers: IQM fully entangles 54 qubits, Pasqal 256 qubits with unexpected physics

*29 October — [The Quantum Insider on IQM](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/29/iqm-full-chip-entanglement-54-qubit-processor/); 27 October — [Pasqal](https://www.pasqal.com/newsroom/pasqals-neutral-atom-processor-uncovers-quantum-magnet-behavior-that-theory-did-not-predict/)*

- **What:** IQM entangled all 54 qubits of Emerald (reported GHZ fidelity 0.516) and is making the full chip available to cloud users. On 256 neutral atoms, Pasqal observed a slowdown in a 2D quantum magnet that neither theory nor classical simulation predicted.
- **Why it matters:** the Pasqal result is the more interesting signal — a result that was not predicted classically is the first type of evidence pointing towards useful quantum simulation. IQM's fidelity of 0.516 is just above the threshold; impressive in scale, modest in quality. Both are European machines, which is relevant for sovereignty arguments.

### 7. PsiQuantum brings quantum photonics to AI data centres

*reported 30 October — [PsiQuantum](https://www.psiquantum.com/news-import/psiquantum-unveils-optical-circuit-switch-technology-built-on-advanced-low-loss-silicon-photonics-platform)*

- **What:** an 8×8 optical circuit switch with about 1.2 dB typical insertion loss on a low-loss silicon photonics platform, aimed at data centre interconnect.
- **Why it matters:** a quantum company turning its photonics technology into AI infrastructure revenue. That is the most realistic commercial path for the entire enabling layer: quantum as technology driver, AI data centres as the sales market.
- **Relevance:** immediately recognisable for the Brabant photonics and packaging chain. The argument "invest in quantum components" becomes much stronger if the near-term market is data centre optics rather than quantum computers.

### 8. Warning signal: private quantum funding cools while share prices hold up

*[Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/quantum-computing-startup-investment-data-quantinuum-ipo/)*

- **What:** Crunchbase reports that VC inflow into quantum startups is declining in 2026, while listed quantum companies remain strong. The week itself confirms that picture: one mid-sized European round (QuantrolOx, €12M) against a billion-dollar acquisition and a series of MoUs.
- **Why it matters:** capital is concentrating in listed companies and corporate M&A, not in new early-stage hardware. That is exactly the stage at which regions with public co-investment and facilities make the difference — and at which companies without a clear route to market become vulnerable.
- **Relevance:** relevant for the Dutch pipeline, which saw several large rounds in 2026 (QuantWare €152M, Groove Quantum €16M, OrangeQS €15M). Deserves monitoring: are these the last rounds of a generous cycle, or the start of a more selective phase?

## 2. Opportunities and calls

### EuroHPC JU — six quantum calls, €119 million

*deadline 17 November 2026, 17:00 CET — [qt.eu overview](https://qt.eu/funding-opportunities/)*

- TIPT-09 trapped ion (€20M), SPT-10 superconducting (€20M), NAPT-11 neutral atoms (€20M), NQKD-12 next-gen QKD and hybrid QKD-PQC (€24M), QTI-13 open-access test infrastructure (€20M), QEXP-14 experimental pilot lines TRL 4–6 (€15M). Consortia; QTI-13 explicitly asks for attention to SMEs and startups, NQKD-12 for the involvement of national network operators.

### CHIPS JU — Quantum Chips calls

*deadline 1 December 2026, 17:00; FPP phase until 31 March 2027 — [qt.eu](https://qt.eu/funding-opportunities/)*

- HORIZON-JU-CHIPS-2026-QUANTUM-1-RIA (quantum chip design and design tools) and QUANTUM-2-RIA (enabling technologies). Of the EU instruments, this fits best with the Brabant combination of semiconductor design, photonics and advanced packaging.

### EuroHPC Quantum Machine Learning (QML-07)

*deadline 28 January 2027, 17:00 — [EuroHPC JU](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/quantum-machine-learning_en)*

- Longer preparation time; suitable for consortium building with application partners from the region.

### EuroHPC MeluXina-Q procurement (Luxembourg)

*[EuroHPC JU](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/eurohpc-ju-launches-procurement-quantum-computer-luxembourg-2026-07-23_en)*

- Tender for a quantum computer of about €12M. Relevant as a supply opportunity for Dutch component and test suppliers.

### NWO "Safety and Resilience in the Quantum Age"

*deadline 3 November 2026, 14:00 — [Holland High Tech](https://www.hollandhightech.nl/en/news-calendar/news/call-for-proposals-safety-and-resilience-in-the-quantum-age)*

- More than €4.5M; portable entanglement €1.0–1.5M per project, PQC up to €385k. *Note:* the mandatory letter of intent already closed on 29 September, so this call is effectively closed to new participants. Mentioned as a signal: defence-related quantum security is now a Dutch funding line in its own right — and it will come back.

### Looking ahead

- HORIZON-CL4-2027-SPACE-03-71 (quantum space gravimetry) opens March 2027, deadline 2 September 2027. And: the European Commission is working on the **Quantum Act**; the public consultation has closed and the proposal is expected ([EC Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-invites-contributions-shape-future-eu-quantum-act)). Positioning regional infrastructure claims has more effect now than after publication.

## 3. Capital, companies and talent

### Acquisitions and investments

- **Intel – Classiq, ~$2.3 billion** *(26 October — [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/intel-to-acquire-classiq/))*: largest quantum acquisition to date; completion in the first half of 2027.
- **QuantrolOx, €12 million Series A2** *(reported 22–30 October — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/27/quantrolox-raises-e12-million-in-series-a2/), [tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/10/22/anglo-finnish-oxford-university-quantum-spinout-raises-eur12m/))*: Anglo-Finnish Oxford spin-out for automated qubit tuning. A small amount, but exactly the "industrialisation of hardware" layer where Dutch players such as OrangeQS and Qblox also sit — and therefore a direct benchmark.
- **New York State** *(30 October — [Governor's Office](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-new-study-highlighting-new-yorks-position-national-leader-quantum))*: a landscape study ranks NY second nationally; $360M of planned investment linked to $2.3–5 billion of projected economic activity. A useful benchmark for how other regions substantiate their quantum claim.

### Partnerships and adoption

- **IonQ – AFRL, $7.5M follow-on funding** *(28 October — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/28/ionq-7-5-million-afrl-full-stack-quantum-networking/))*: expansion of the quantum network in Rome, New York with free-space optics and frequency conversion; follows an earlier $21.1M collaboration.
- **IonQ QKD in Florida** *(30 October — [Palm Beach State College](https://news.pbsc.edu/stories/2026/palm-beach-state-college-announces-launch-of-first-segment-of-the-nations-first-statewide-quantum-safe-network.php))*: first live segment of a statewide quantum-safe network over the county's dark fibre.
- **D-Wave – LG CNS** *(30 October — [D-Wave IR](https://ir.dwavequantum.com/news/news-details/2026/LG-CNS-and-D-Wave-Announce-Strategic-Relationship-to-Advance-Quantum-Optimization-Adoption-in-South-Korea/default.aspx))*: non-binding MoU for annealing optimisation in South Korean industry, telecom, finance and retail.
- **Quantinuum – GE Vernova** *([Quantinuum](https://www.quantinuum.com/blog/quantinuum-and-ge-vernova-collaborate-to-advance-high-temperature-superconductivity-research))*: quantum simulation for high-temperature superconductivity. One of the few collaborations with a plausible industrial end product.
- **SoftBank and five Japanese partners** *(28 October — [The Qubit Report](https://thequbitreport.com/software-algorithms/2026/10/29/softbank-and-partners-launch-nedo-quantum-materials-platform/))*: NEDO-backed quantum materials platform.
- **SEALSQ – WiseSat** *([SEALSQ](https://www.sealsq.com/investors/news-releases/sealsq-announces-intent-to-acquire-capacity-and-operational-services-associated-with-12-wisesat.space-satellites-for-qsoc))*: intention to acquire capacity on 12 satellites for quantum-safe services.

### Dutch context

- **QuTech leads European quantum internet prototype** *(16 October — [QuTech](https://qutech.nl/2026/10/16/qutech-coordinates-european-effort-to-build-worlds-first-full-stack-quantum-internet-prototype/), [QIA via qt.eu](https://qt.eu/news/2026/2026-10-08_QIA-enters-new-phase-to-build-full-stack-quantum-networks))*: next phase of the Quantum Internet Alliance, €47.5M. Just outside the weekly window, but the contrast with the IonQ–AFRL model is the real signal: Europe funds research, the US funds deployment with a defence customer. The Netherlands has public money for the network, but hardly a market yet.
- **TU Delft** *([TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2026/tnw/delft-researchers-control-the-magnetic-core-of-a-single-atom-against-expectations))*: control over the magnetic core of a single atom, against expectations. Fundamental, relevant for quantum sensing in the longer term.
