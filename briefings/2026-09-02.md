*Strategic signals from quantum computing, networking, sensing and the supply chain — filtered for relevance to Brainport, Brabant, the Netherlands and Europe.*

**The essence.** This week Europe twice handed out money for the same problem — getting hardware to market — while the rest of the world showed how big the stakes are. EuroHPC selected 13 QPU startups for the Quantum Grand Challenge, including **two Dutch ones** (Groove Quantum, QuiX Quantum), with a route to up to €30M of EIB venture debt per company. At the same time Canada put CAD 195M behind a photonic quantum factory in one go, Pasqal went to Nasdaq via a SPAC with $360M in cash, and IBM completed its acquisition of HRL Labs — silicon spin qubits, exactly the domain where Delft is strong. The underlying movement: *money is shifting from qubits to fabrication, interconnect and control* — the layer where Brainport actually has something to offer.

## 1. Top signals

### 1. EuroHPC selects 13 European QPU startups — two Dutch among them

*26 August 2026 — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/eurohpc-joint-undertaking-selects-13-european-qpu-startups-for-quantum-grand-challenge/) · [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/26/sparrow-quantum-eurohpc-quantum-grand-challenge-funding/)*

*What:* The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking announced the selection for the Quantum Grand Challenge. Of 30 submissions (27 eligible), 13 were selected, plus 3 on a reserve list; the Governing Board approved the selection on 19 June. Phase 1 gives each project about **€300,000** for a four-month engagement (~€3.87M in total). Companies that complete phase 1 gain access to phase 2: up to **€30 million in individual venture debt per company** from a €100M InvestEU pool via the EIB. Selected: Alice & Bob, Quandela, QUOBLY (FR), eleQtron, NVISION, QUDORA (DE), IQM (FI), Equal 1 (IE), Planckian (IT), Qilimanjaro (ES), Sparrow Quantum (DK) and **Groove Quantum** and **QuiX Quantum** (NL).

*Why it matters:* This is the first EU instrument that explicitly links a grant to scale-up capital rather than to yet another research project. The €300k is symbolic; the €30M of venture debt is not. It is Europe's answer to the question of why European quantum companies raise their growth capital in the US.

*For Brainport:* Two Dutch names out of thirteen is strong — but both are outside Brabant (Delft and Enschede). The relevant question for Brainport is not who is on the list, but who will soon *manufacture* for these companies: if QuiX and Groove move towards EIB venture debt and volume production in 2027, they will need fab capacity, packaging, cryogenic assembly and test infrastructure. That is Brainport territory. This is a concrete starting point for outreach.

### 2. IBM completes acquisition of HRL Laboratories — adding silicon spin qubits

*26 August 2026 — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/26/ibm-completes-acquisition-hrl-laboratories/) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ibm-completes-acquisition-of-hrl-laboratories-to-accelerate-the-future-of-quantum-302859819.html) · [IBM newsroom (announcement 23 July)](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-23-ibm-to-acquire-hrl-laboratories-to-power-the-future-of-quantum)*

*What:* IBM has completed the acquisition of HRL Laboratories (previously owned by Boeing and GM). With it, IBM adds silicon spin qubit expertise to its existing superconducting line and moves to an explicitly multi-modality roadmap, tied to its own foundry capacity.

*Why it matters:* IBM is hedging its technology choice. Silicon spin qubits are attractive because they can in principle be made on existing CMOS lines — the bridge between quantum and the semiconductor industry. That a US hyperscaler is now buying this in rather than building it itself says something about the value of scarce spin qubit teams.

*For Brainport/NL:* Silicon spin qubits are exactly where QuTech/Delft leads internationally, and the fabrication route runs via CMOS — and thus via the Dutch semiconductor chain. Two implications: (a) growing competition for Dutch spin qubit talent and IP, with acquisition risk for Dutch spin-offs; (b) an opening, because if spin qubits become the industrial route, the Brabant semicon chain is strategically more relevant than with superconducting qubits. Worth watching whom IBM approaches next.

### 3. Pasqal goes to Nasdaq via SPAC — $360M in cash, share price nearly doubles

*28–29 August 2026 — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/28/pasqal-completes-spac-merger-with-360-million-in-cash/) · [TQI (debut)](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/29/pasqal-shares-nearly-double-in-nasdaq-debut/) · [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/pasqal-completes-business-combination-with-bleichroeder-acquisition-corp-ii-begins-trading-on-nasdaq/)*

*What:* French neutral-atom company Pasqal completed its business combination with Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. II and has traded since 28 August as PSQL (warrants: PSQLW), with about **$360 million** available at closing at a valuation of ~$2 billion. The share rose sharply on the first trading day. Pasqal has deployed 7 QPUs with 3 in production, and says it will use the money for scaling production, the step to fault tolerance and HPC integration.

*Why it matters:* One of Europe's flagships is raising its growth capital on a US exchange, via a SPAC, at a multiple of roughly 100x revenue. That is both good news (European hardware is financeable) and a warning (European capital markets do not deliver this, and the valuation is fragile). Note: Xanadu, EigenQ ($3B SPAC in preparation) and Quantum Space are following the same route.

*For NL:* Relevant as a benchmark for QuantWare (€152M private, May 2026) and for the debate about the Dutch €9.4bn scale-up gap. The question this raises: where will Dutch quantum companies raise their post-Series B rounds — and do we accept that this will be in New York?

### 4. Canada puts CAD 195M behind a CAD 893M photonic quantum factory

*28 August 2026 — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/28/3352847/0/en/xanadu-secures-cad-195-million-and-establishes-landmark-advanced-photonics-facility.html) · [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/canada-commits-cad-195m-140-2-m-usd-to-xanadu-for-893m-642-2m-usd-inception-quantum-manufacturing-facility/) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/xanadu-secures-cad-195m-for-quantum-manufacturing-expansion/)*

*What:* The Canadian government commits CAD 195M (~$140M) to Xanadu for "Inception", an advanced photonics facility in Toronto with a total project size of CAD 893M (~$642M) and about 275 jobs. In the same week Xanadu published a roadmap towards [1,000+ logical qubits in 2031](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/31/xanadu-1000-logical-qubits-2031/) (31 August), with network-linked fault-tolerant quantum data centres in 2029–2030.

*Why it matters:* This is industrial policy, not a research grant: one country, one company, one factory, three quarters of a billion. The scale is an order of magnitude above what Europe does per case (compare the €50M P4Q pilot line for all of Europe).

*For Brainport:* Directly competing with the Dutch proposition. Integrated photonics is exactly where Brainport stakes its claim — TNO's InP pilot line on the High Tech Campus (with ASML lithography since June 2026), PITC, PhotonDelta, PIXEurope. Canada is now buying in one go what Brainport has built over ten years. Argument for the Dutch and European conversation: step changes in photonic manufacturing require national commitments in the hundreds of millions, not tens.

### 5. Quantum sensing becomes operational — and defence pays

*27 & 31 August 2026 — [Q-CTRL](https://q-ctrl.com/blog/q-ctrl-achieves-worlds-first-gps-free-quantum-gravimetric-navigation-demonstration-in-maritime-field-trial) · [GPS World](https://www.gpsworld.com/q-ctrl-demonstrates-gps-free-quantum-navigation-in-coral-sea-field-trial/) · [Infleqtion](https://infleqtion.com/nasa-awards-infleqtion-20-million-as-worlds-first-quantum-gravity-mission-advances-toward-flight/) · [QCR (Diffraqtion)](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/diffraqtion-caps-pre-seed-round-at-10m-with-strategic-backing-from-lockheed-martin-and-presidio-ventures/)*

*What:* Three signals in one week. (a) Q-CTRL demonstrated the first GPS-free quantum gravimetric maritime navigation in a field trial in the Coral Sea, with a bounded position error of about 1 nautical mile (27 Aug). (b) NASA awarded Infleqtion a $20M follow-on contract for the Quantum Gravity Gradiometer Pathfinder mission, bringing the total to $40M (27 Aug). (c) Diffraqtion (Somerville, MA) closed a $10M+ pre-seed with **Lockheed Martin Ventures** and Presidio Ventures for quantum-optical cameras for space domain awareness (31 Aug).

*Why it matters:* Sensing is the first quantum branch stepping out of the lab into the field, and the buyer is defence and space — not the commercial market. That means shorter procurement routes, different requirements (ruggedised, manufacturable, certified) and different funders (corporate venture arms of defence primes).

*For Brabant:* The relevant question is not whether the Netherlands builds a gravimeter, but who supplies the *components*: lasers, vacuum systems, optical benches, vibration isolation, precision mechatronics. That is the classic Brabant supplier strength, with a clear defence procurement demand behind it. Linking to the ongoing Dutch defence innovation agenda is obvious — QuiX Quantum was previously selected for the [Purple NECtar Defence Innovation Challenge](https://www.quixquantum.com/news/quix-quantum-selected-to-participate-in-purple-nectar-defence-innovation-challenge).

### 6. Money is moving to the control and interconnect layer

*31 August 2026 — [VTT](https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/s-transistors-raises-eu26-million-pre-seed-round-introduce-unique-superconducting) · [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/08/finlands-s-transistors-raises-e2-6-million-to-build-the-worlds-first-quantum-motherboard) · [QCR (QTREX)](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qtrex-quantum-to-unveil-17280-line-cryogenic-interconnect-architecture-at-ieee-quantum-week-2026/)*

*What:* S-Transistors (VTT spin-out, Espoo) raised a **€2.6M pre-seed** for superconducting "quantum motherboards" — wafer-scale control electronics that sit inside the cryostat itself, to get around the cabling bottleneck. In the same week QTREX Quantum (Nasdaq: QTEX) announced a cryogenic interconnect architecture supporting up to 17,280 coaxial lines per cryogenic stage — more than double any previously disclosed architecture for commercial dilution refrigerators — made with additively manufactured electronics (AME). Demonstration at IEEE Quantum Week 2026 in Toronto, 13–18 September.

*Why it matters:* The scaling problem of quantum computers has long ceased to be a qubit problem; it is a cabling, cooling and control problem. That is a *manufacturing* problem, not a physics problem — and early-stage capital is now going into it.

*For Brainport:* This is the most underestimated opportunity in this briefing. Cryogenic interconnect, high-density packaging, additive electronics and precision assembly are not quantum competences — they are high-tech manufacturing competences that are amply available in Brabant. A Brabant supplier does not need to become a quantum company to take a position here. It deserves a targeted exploration of which regional players (contract manufacturers, packaging, cryogenic engineering) could bid for this.

### 7. Warning signal: Italian court resets €61.2M quantum tender

*26–28 August 2026 — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/28/italian-court-upholds-halt-to-ibms-e61-million-campania-quantum-contract/) · [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/italian-council-of-state-rejects-region-appeal-resetting-e61-2m-campania-quantum-tender/)*

*What:* The Italian Council of State rejected the appeal of the Campania region, definitively taking the award of a €61.2M quantum contract to IBM off the table and leaving Tea Tek as the only valid bidder. It is the second time an Italian court has annulled an IBM quantum award (previously €47M at the University of Salerno).

*Why it matters:* Regional governments are procuring quantum systems with tender frameworks that are not designed for them: overly specific requirements lead to de facto single-sourcing, and that does not hold up. Two years of delay and reputational damage are the result.

*For us:* A direct lesson for any regional or national quantum procurement in the Netherlands. Anyone tendering a quantum facility must formulate functional rather than technology-specific requirements, or run the same risk.

### 8. US: bill places quantum with Commerce and focuses on the supply chain

*31 August / 1 September 2026 — [Rep. Langworthy](https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-langworthy-introduces-american-quantum-competitiveness-act) · [Congress.gov (H.R. 10163)](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/10163/cosponsors)*

*What:* The American Quantum Competitiveness Act (H.R. 10163) designates the Department of Commerce as the lead agency for commercial quantum technology, with explicit attention to *supply chain resilience*.

*Why it matters:* It frames quantum as an industrial and trade policy file, not a science file. That is the same pattern we saw with semiconductors — with export controls as the predictable sequel. Contrast with Europe: the [EU Quantum Act](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-new-plan-for-europe-s-sustainable-prosperity-and-competitiveness/file-quantum-act) was planned for Q2 2026 and still has not appeared.

*For NL:* Dutch suppliers in the quantum chain must take into account a tightening US regime around quantum components. Worth raising early with regional companies that still think this does not affect them.

## 2. Opportunities and calls

### EuroHPC — six quantum calls, €119M in total

*(Deadline: 17 November 2026, 17:00 CET — [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/24/eurohpc-119m-funding-calls-quantum-technologies/) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/eurohpc-launches-6-quantum-calls-with-e119m-in-funding/) · [EU Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-JU-EUROHPC-2026-NQKD-12-01))*

Published 24 August, just outside the window, but the most action-oriented item in this briefing.

- Trapped-ion platform — €20M (HORIZON-JU-EUROHPC-2026-TIPT-09)
- Superconducting platform — €20M (SPT-10)
- Neutral-atom platform — €20M (NAPT-11)
- Next-generation QKD systems — €24M (NQKD-12)
- **Quantum testing infrastructure — €20M (QTI-13)**
- **Quantum experimental pilot lines — €15M (QEXP-14)**

The last two are the most promising for Brainport: testing infrastructure and experimental pilot lines connect to the TNO InP pilot line on the High Tech Campus, PITC and the PIXEurope line. Ten weeks to the deadline means a consortium decision has to be made this month.

### NWO / Holland High Tech — "Safety and Resilience in the Quantum Age"

*(Letter of intent: 29 September 2026 · Full proposal: 3 November 2026, 14:00 — [Holland High Tech](https://www.hollandhightech.nl/en/news-calendar/news/call-for-proposals-safety-and-resilience-in-the-quantum-age))*

More than €4.5M available. Two lines: portable entanglement in quantum networks (€1–1.5M per project) and post-quantum cryptography (max. €385k per project). Researchers individually or in a consortium with societal partners; oriented towards defence and security. Information session 15 September. **The LOI deadline is in four weeks** — this is the next hard date.

### EuroHPC Quantum Grand Challenge phase 2 — EIB venture debt

*(ongoing — [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/eurohpc-joint-undertaking-selects-13-european-qpu-startups-for-quantum-grand-challenge/))*

€100M InvestEU pool, up to €30M per company, accessible to the 13 selected companies after completing phase 1 (four months). For Groove Quantum and QuiX Quantum this means a capital window in the first half of 2027 — and therefore a production and supply demand that is already emerging now.

### EIC Transition Open

*(Deadline: 16 September 2026 — [HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN](https://www.euacc.ai/calls/HORIZON-EIC-2026-TRANSITIONOPEN))*

Not quantum-specific, but relevant for deeptech spin-outs that want to move from TRL 4 to 6. Deadline in two weeks.

### To watch

The **EU Quantum Act** was in the Commission work programme for Q2 2026 and has not yet been published ([Legislative Train](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-new-plan-for-europe-s-sustainable-prosperity-and-competitiveness/file-quantum-act)). Expected content: coordination of R&I investment between member states, industrial capacity for quantum manufacturing, and security of quantum supply chains. That third point may directly affect Brabant suppliers.

## 3. Capital, companies and talent

### Listings and consolidation

*(26–31 August 2026)*

Pasqal lists on Nasdaq as PSQL with ~$360M in cash ([TQI](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/28/pasqal-completes-spac-merger-with-360-million-in-cash/)). IBM completes the HRL acquisition ([TQI](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/26/ibm-completes-acquisition-hrl-laboratories/)). EigenQ is pushing ahead with its $3 billion SPAC merger with Silicon Valley Acquisition Corp via an S-4, with completion expected in Q4 2026 ([QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/eigenq-and-silicon-valley-acquisition-corp-advance-3b-spac-merger-via-form-s-4-submission/)). A clear pattern is emerging: European and US quantum companies are going to US exchanges via SPACs, at valuations far ahead of revenue.

### Early rounds — all in the supplier layer

*(27–31 August 2026)*

S-Transistors €2.6M pre-seed for superconducting control motherboards ([VTT](https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/s-transistors-raises-eu26-million-pre-seed-round-introduce-unique-superconducting)) · Diffraqtion $10M+ pre-seed with Lockheed Martin Ventures and Presidio Ventures for quantum-optical cameras ([QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/diffraqtion-caps-pre-seed-round-at-10m-with-strategic-backing-from-lockheed-martin-and-presidio-ventures/)) · ColibriTD €4M seed for multiphysics quantum simulation software, Paris, led by Earlybird ([QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/colibritd-raises-e4-million-4-66-million-usd-seed-round-to-scale-multiphysics-quantum-simulation-software/)). Notably, none of these three is building a quantum computer. The capital is going to what surrounds it.

### Partnerships and deployment

*(28–31 August 2026)*

Diraq is installing an eight-qubit silicon quantum computer in an Equinix data centre in Sydney (October 2026) and opening an engineering hub in Santa Monica · Pasqal and KACST start a collaboration on post-quantum cryptography in Saudi Arabia ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/31/3353273/0/en/pasqal-and-kacst-launch-strategic-quantum-research-collaboration-as-pasqal-expands-presence-in-saudi-arabia.html)) · Anyon Systems and Matrix Group focus on government and defence deployment · Yaqumo and SCREEN Holdings are building optical modules for neutral-atom QPUs under a NEDO project ([QCR news overview](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)). The common thread: quantum systems are moving from labs to data centres, and governments outside Europe are paying for it.

### Talent and market sentiment

Appointments at Quantum Sky, IonQ, Pasqal and Global Quantum Intelligence, including new Chief Growth Officers — a sign that commercialisation now ranks above technology on the agenda (29 Aug, [QCR](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)). At the same time, quantum stocks are moving strongly with the broader risk-off movement in speculative tech, and analysts point to the sector's vulnerability in a correction ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4544346-quantum-computing-stocks-hit-hard-as-tech-turns-risk-off), [Motley Fool, 1 Sept](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/01/perdiction-if-a-september-sell-off-hits-speculativ/)). That is commentary, not fact — but it is the climate in which Dutch companies will have to raise their next round in 2027.
